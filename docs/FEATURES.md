# What Hearth does today

A reference for the functionality that exists and works, as of 2026-09-04. For status and roadmap see
`PLAN.md`; for how the pieces fit see `ARCHITECTURE.md`; for the wire format see `PROTOCOL.md`.

## Running it

| Command | What you get |
|---|---|
| `./play.sh` | Game window + brain. Jonah on the local model, others scripted, voices on. Closing the game stops the brain. |
| `./play.sh --brain scripted --voice none` | Silent, free, no AI. Good for map work. |
| `./play.sh --brain claude --ai-agents jonah --budget 2` | Jonah on Claude, stops at $2 (needs `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`). |
| `cd brain && .venv/bin/python -m hearth run --brain scripted --voice say --ticks 200` | Headless: just the voices, no Unreal. |
| `cd brain && .venv/bin/pytest` | 21 tests, ~1 s. |

Brain flags: `--brain claude|ollama|scripted`, `--ai-agents jonah,mara`, `--voice say|none`, `--tick-seconds N`
(real seconds per 10 world minutes; 6 is good with Unreal, 0 is max speed), `--ticks N`, `--agents N`,
`--model`, `--effort`, `--budget USD`, `--ollama-model`, `--thoughts` (print private reasoning), `--quiet`,
`--no-ws`, `--port`, `--seed`. Env: `HEARTH_MODEL`, `HEARTH_EFFORT`, `HEARTH_OLLAMA_MODEL`, `HEARTH_WS_PORT`.

## In the game

**You** are a third-person mannequin. WASD walk, mouse look, Shift run, **Space** jump (tap for a hop, hold for full
height; falls a little heavier than it rises). You start a short walk from camp. No flying.

**ESC** opens the menu: Resume, Quit to Desktop, look-sensitivity slider shown as 1–10 in half steps (persists). ESC again resumes. Quit closes the game; the brain notices the disconnect and exits too.

**Talking.** Characters with a cyan name, a floating `[ AI ]` tag, and a glow are model-driven. Within
about 7 m of one, "Press ENTER to talk to <name>" appears. ENTER opens a chat box: type, Enter to send.
The reply appears in the box, above their head, and is spoken in their voice. ESC (or walking away)
ends it. While you talk they stop and wait; afterwards they remember what you said and go back to work.
If you go quiet for two minutes they leave.

**Gathering.** Stand at the Forest, River, Meadow, or Quarry and the top-left HUD counts down 10 s, then
you're handed one wood / fish / berries / stone (the place's stock goes down, the people nearby see it).
Stay longer, get more. Walk into camp carrying anything and it's dropped into the shared stockpile
automatically. "Carrying: ..." is always shown top-left.

**Cheat codes.** Type `hump` while playing (not in the chat box): your character dances and humps the
air for 6 seconds. More can be added in `HearthPlayerController::OnCheatKey`.

**Labels.** Every character shows name and current action (and "hungry"/"cold" etc. when a need is
low). Every place shows a label with what's there: resources, fire state, shelter progress, stockpile.

**The world.** A ~100 m valley. Camp (fire pit, logs, stockpile, shelter site) in the middle; Forest
(wood), River (water, fish), Meadow (berries), Quarry (stone) around it. Placeholder shapes and flat
colors, generated by script. Camp fire has a point light that turns on when lit.

## The AI's instructions (editable)

Everything the models are told lives in plain files in `brain/prompts/`: `world_rules.md` (the
baseline: who they are, the situation, how to behave), `personas.toml` (each person), `converse_rules.md`
(talking to you), `reflection.md`, `local_model_hint.md` (extra guidance for the small local model).
Edit, restart the brain. See `brain/prompts/README.md`.

## The simulation (brain)

**Time.** One tick = 10 world minutes. Night is 21:00–06:00. A day is 144 ticks (about 14 real minutes
at `--tick-seconds 6`).

**Weather.** clear / overcast / rain / cold / storm, rolled every 1–4 hours. Rain, cold, and night drain
warmth; the fire and (once built) the shelter counter it.

**People.** Six personas (`brain/prompts/personas.toml`, editable): Mara (farmer, organizer), Jonah (city guy,
nervous, funny), Teodora (retired nurse, observant), Ravi (hiker, few words), Lena (art student,
cheerful), Oscar (contractor, gruff). Each has a macOS voice and a body type (Manny/Quinn).

**Needs.** hunger, thirst, energy, warmth, health (0–100). Working drains faster. Any need at 0 drains
health; health 0 = dead. Sleeping restores energy; eating/drinking restore hunger/thirst.

**Actions.** wait, move_to, gather, eat, drink, rest, sleep, tend_fire, build, deposit, take, give.
Actions take 1–36 ticks. Invalid actions become a wait and the character is told why next time.

**Camp.** Fire burns 1 hour per wood. Shelter needs 8 work sessions of 1 wood + 1 stone. The stockpile
is shared; `deposit` puts everything you carry in it, `take` pulls from it.

**Speech.** A line is heard by everyone at the same place. Addressing someone by name lets them reply
next tick even mid-rest. Nobody speaks twice within 4 ticks unless replying. Spoken aloud via TTS,
one line at a time; the world waits for the line to finish.

**Memory.** Last 30 things seen/heard, a plan note to self, and (model brains) periodic reflections
compressing older memory into a few lines.

**Brains.**
- *Scripted*: rules with roles (Mara wood, Jonah water, ...), emergencies first, shares food, goes home
  at night, ~40 canned lines used sparingly. $0.
- *Ollama*: `qwen2.5:7b` locally. JSON-schema decisions, conversation with the visitor, opener
  de-repetition, repeat penalty. ~1–3 s per reply on the M3 Pro. $0.
- *Claude*: `claude-opus-5` by default, effort low, cached system prompt, structured output, refusal
  handling, per-run cost estimate, `--budget` stop.
- *Mixed*: any subset of people on a model, the rest scripted.

**God mode** (over the WebSocket, see PROTOCOL.md): `storm`, `drop_supplies`, `whisper`, `pause`, `resume`.

## Unreal project (`unreal/Hearth`)

- `HearthBridgeSubsystem`: WebSocket client; keeps latest world state; delegates for init, snapshot,
  speech, event, reply; auto-reconnect; `SendCommand`, `SendTalk`, `SendTalkEnd`.
- `HearthGameMode`: spawns locations and people from `world_init`; applies snapshots; places the visitor.
- `AHearthAgent`: mannequin body chosen by `body` (AI people get a forest-green suit); labels; nav movement at a speed matched to the sim;
  AI tag/glow; velocity-driven idle/walk/jog clips (`HearthAnim.h`).
- `AHearthVisitor`: the player; third-person camera; look sensitivity from the controller; guards against
  the first-frame mouse jump.
- `AHearthPlayerController`: proximity prompt, SPACE chat (Slate), ESC menu, sensitivity setting
  saved to the user's Game.ini, gathering countdown + carry HUD.
- `AHearthLocation`: marker + label + fire light per place.
- `Scripts/build_valley_map.py`: regenerates the whole level and its materials from scratch.
- Everything builds and runs from the command line; the editor UI is optional.
